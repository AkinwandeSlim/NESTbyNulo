### Questions & Discussion (15 minutes)

"Those are the three options. Before you decide, what questions do you have?"

**[Listen carefully, take notes]**

**Anticipated questions & answers:**

**Q: "What happened to the original ₦100M property?"**  
A: "We identified several candidates, but without the full ₦100M committed, sellers won't hold properties for us. We need to either raise the remaining ₦85M first, or pivot to a property we can buy with what we have now."

**Q: "Why didn't you tell us sooner about the delay?"**  
A: "That's fair feedback, and I take responsibility for not communicating more frequently. We've been heads-down trying to solve the problem rather than keeping you updated while solving it. That's why I'm calling everyone personally now."

**Q: "Can you guarantee you'll raise the ₦85M by December if I choose Option A?"**  
A: "No, I can't guarantee it. That's why I'm being transparent about the risk and offering the automatic pivot/refund if we don't hit it by December 31."

**Q: "What if the ₦18M property is in a bad location?"**  
A: "You'll approve the specific property before we close. I'll send you full details: address, photos, current tenant info, rental history, valuation report. If you don't approve, we find another one or you can switch to refund."

**Q: "What returns are you promising?"**  
A: "I'm not promising specific returns - that would be misleading. I'm sharing expected ranges based on market rates for similar properties (12-15% yield). Actual returns depend on occupancy, rent collection, property management, and market conditions. The full risk disclosure will be in the written investment memo."

**Q: "Is NEST legal? Are you registered with SEC?"**  
A: "Great question. We're using an SPV (Special Purpose Vehicle) structure, which is the correct legal entity for fractional property ownership in Nigeria. We're currently consulting with securities counsel on whether SEC registration is required for our specific structure. That legal opinion will be complete before we close any property, and you'll see it."

**Q: "What if I need my money back in 6 months?"**  
A: "Real estate is illiquid - that's the nature of property investment. There's a minimum lock-up period (we'll specify this clearly in the investment agreement - likely 12-24 months). After that, we're building a secondary market where you can sell your shares to other investors. But if you think you'll need the money within 6 months, Option C (refund now) might be better for you."

**Q: "Why should I trust you after this delay?"**  
A: "You shouldn't trust me based on promises - you should trust me based on what I do next. That's why I'm offering the refund option with no penalty. If you give me another chance, I'll prove we can execute by closing the ₦18M property in 6 weeks and getting you rental income by November. Track record speaks louder than words."
