## Risk Disclosures (Show in Demo)

### Property-Specific Risks
1. **Vacancy Risk:** Property may remain unoccupied for periods, generating no income
2. **Tenant Default:** Tenants may fail to pay rent on time or in full
3. **Property Depreciation:** Market value may decrease due to economic conditions
4. **Maintenance Costs:** Unexpected repairs may reduce net income
5. **Market Risk:** Real estate market downturn in Lokogoma/Abuja area

### Investment Risks
1. **Illiquidity:** Cannot withdraw investment for 24-month lock-up period
2. **Platform Risk:** NEST is a new platform with limited operational history
3. **Regulatory Risk:** Changes in securities laws may affect operations
4. **No Guarantee:** Returns are projected estimates, not guaranteed
5. **Loss of Capital:** Possible to lose some or all of invested capital
