One Property. Many Owners. Shared Returns.

NEST acquires a NGN 100 million property in Lekki, Lagos through a legally registered Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV). The property is divided into 200 units of NGN 500,000 each. You buy as many units as you want — 1 unit, 10 units, or 50 units.

Each unit entitles you to an equal share of rental income and property appreciation. Your investment is legally protected by the SPV structure — the same structure used by institutional real estate funds worldwide.
