**Challenge on Original BMC:**

**Question Asked:**
- Pick ONE primary customer segment to start with
- Describe a specific person: age, location, income, current behavior, specific pain point
- What is the SIZE of this segment in your target market?

**TEAM'S ANSWER:**
**Customer: Lucia Omonokhua**
- **Role:** NGO Program Officer
- **Age:** 34
- **Location:** Wuse 2, Abuja
- **Income:** ₦850,000/month
- **Savings:** ₦2.1M sitting in fixed deposit
- **Current Behavior:** Does ajo/contribution savings with trusted network
- **Specific Pain:** Can't get into income-generating real estate because entry cost runs ₦50M+, while her savings sit in an account losing real value to inflation every month she waits

**Advisor Assessment:**
✅ **MUCH BETTER.** This is a specific person with a real problem. But note: Lucia is NOT "financially excluded" or "underserved youth" - she's a middle-income professional with ₦2.1M saved. This means NEST is actually targeting **middle-class investment access**, not mass-market financial inclusion.

**Key Insight:** The BMC says "youth 15-35" but Lucia represents 30-45 year old professionals earning ₦500K-₦1M/month with ₦1M-₦5M saved. These are DIFFERENT businesses with different unit economics and positioning.
