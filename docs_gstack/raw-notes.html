Regulatory Note Added September 16, 2026

The discovery session did not address the Nigerian Investment and Securities Act (ISA) 2025, which replaced the ISA 2007 in March 2025. The ISA 2025 expands SEC Nigeria's oversight to include: - A broader definition of "securities" that may include fractional property interests - Stricter regulation of collective investment schemes (CIS) - Expanded oversight of SPVs used to issue investment-related securities No legal advice is provided here. All regulatory items require confirmation by qualified Nigerian securities and property counsel. See requirements.md Section 7 for the full regulatory context and pre-live checklist.
